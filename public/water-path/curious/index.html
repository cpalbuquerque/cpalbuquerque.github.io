Curious things

Plants that break the rules

Some plants do things that look impossible: they dry out and come back to life, or breathe with their roots under water. Each plate here takes one of these puzzles apart in 3D, with the physiology that explains it and the papers it rests on.

Plate I

Back from the dead: resurrection plants

Dry two plants until almost no water is left, watch their cells fold and fill with sugar, then add rain. One takes its chloroplasts apart and rebuilds them; the other keeps them and guards them.

Plate II

Breathing underwater: aerenchyma

Flood a rice or a wheat root and watch air channels open as cells die on purpose. Seal the outer root and see whether oxygen leaks into the mud or reaches the tip.
