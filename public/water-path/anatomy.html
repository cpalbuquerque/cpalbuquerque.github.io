Every mature tissue here belongs to one of three systems: dermal outside, ground in the bulk, vascular for the plumbing. Meristems cut across that: a meristem is a tissue still dividing, and it belongs to whichever system it builds. Tap a region for the mechanism, worked examples, and the reference.

The three simple tissues

The wall is the only real difference between these three. They are not confined to the ground tissue system: xylem and phloem both contain parenchyma, and the fibre caps outside the phloem are sclerenchyma sitting inside a vascular tissue. The dermal and vascular systems also build cells that are none of the three, such as guard cells, sieve elements and vessel elements.

Tissue definitions after Taiz, Zeiger, Møller & Murphy, Plant Physiology and Development, 6th ed. Grapevine petiole after Keller, The Science of Grapevines, 2nd ed., ch. 1. Diagrams are schematic, drawn to teach the arrangement, not to scale within an organ.
